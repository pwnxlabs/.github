# Contributing to pwnxlabs

Thank you for considering a contribution to **pwnxlabs**! We welcome input from the community in the form of code, documentation, tools, or ideas that help expand our understanding of **Lua scripting**, **Roblox internals**, and related research.

> ⚠️ Please read this guide carefully before submitting contributions.

---

## 📝 Scope of Contributions

We accept contributions that align with the technical interests of the organization, including but not limited to:

- Reverse engineering of Lua-based systems (e.g., bytecode analysis)
- Memory inspection and debugging utilities
- Educational tools for learning advanced Lua techniques
- Research into client-side behavior and data structures
- Documentation and toolchain improvements

All work should have a strong emphasis on **learning**, **analysis**, and **technical curiosity**, rather than malicious or exploitative intent.

---

## 🤝 How to Contribute

1. **Fork the repository** you're interested in contributing to.
2. Create a new branch for your feature or bugfix:  
   `git checkout -b feature/your-feature-name`
3. Make your changes, keeping commits clean and descriptive.
4. Ensure any new code includes comments where appropriate.
5. Push your branch to your fork.
6. Open a pull request against the relevant repository in the `pwnxlabs` organization.

Please include a clear description of what your contribution does and why it’s useful.

---

## ⚖️ Legal and Ethical Responsibility

By contributing, you agree that:

- The content you submit is your own original work or properly attributed.
- Your contribution complies with applicable laws and ethical standards.
- You understand that misuse of tools provided in this organization may violate the Terms of Service of platforms like Roblox.

We do **not support or encourage** use of any project for unauthorized access, cheating, or disruption. All contributors are expected to act responsibly.

---

## 🔧 Contribution Guidelines

- Follow the coding style already present in the project.
- If adding new features, document how they work.
- Avoid unnecessary dependencies.
- Clearly describe any experimental or unstable components.
- Respect feedback and be open to revisions.

---

## 🛡️ Reporting Issues

If you find a bug or have a suggestion, please open an issue in the relevant repository.

---

## 👥 Community

We value respectful, constructive, and curious collaboration. Whether you're submitting code, improving documentation, or just asking questions, your participation helps shape this space.
