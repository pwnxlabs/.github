# 🧪 pwnxlabs

A collection of Lua-based tools, scripts, and research related to Roblox development.

---

## 🔎 About

This organization focuses on exploring the capabilities of **Lua scripting** within the **Roblox** platform. Our work includes reverse engineering, game analysis, and the development of custom tools for educational and research purposes.

We believe in transparency, learning through exploration, and responsible use of technical knowledge.
